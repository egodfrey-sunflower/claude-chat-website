A few facts to start with

  • Britain has only around 30–35 native tree species. The ice sheets wiped the slate clean, and rising seas cut the land bridge to Europe about 8,000 years ago before many more could return.
  • The Fortingall Yew in Perthshire is among the oldest living things in Europe, perhaps 2,000–3,000 years old or more.
  • Britain holds roughly half the world's bluebells (Hyacinthoides non-scripta).
  • The Scottish wildcat is Britain's rarest native mammal. Captive-bred cats have been released in the Cairngorms since 2023.
